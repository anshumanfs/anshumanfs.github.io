How this is calculated

Total nutrient need = recommended rate (kg/ha) × area in hectares.

Fertilizer needed (kg) = nutrient need (kg) ÷ nutrient fraction in the selected source.

FAQs

Should all urea be applied at once?
Usually it is split into 2–3 doses by crop stage to reduce losses.

Can soil-test recommendations override this?
Yes. Always prioritise soil-test-based advisory when available.
