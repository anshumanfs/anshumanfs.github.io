Seed Rate Calculator

Estimate seed requirement from plant spacing, germination, purity and thousand-seed weight.

Results

How this is calculated

Plant population = area in square metres ÷ plant geometry (row spacing × plant spacing).

Corrected seeds needed = target population ÷ (germination fraction × purity fraction).

Seed quantity (kg) = corrected seeds × (1000-seed weight in g ÷ 1000) ÷ 1000.
