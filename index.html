About

I'm a full-stack engineer with six years of experience taking systems from design to production: a payment gateway handling 15,000+ transactions a day, ETL pipelines moving 10 billion records a month, and a low-code API platform that cut delivery time from 14 days to 2 hours for 2,000+ data scientists. More recently I've worked on LLM training data and domain-specific AI evaluation with Micro1 AI and Scale AI.

I hold an M.Tech from IIT Kharagpur, and alongside industry work I collaborate on research in precision agriculture: smartphone-based soil sensing, proximal and remote sensor fusion, and generative-AI-assisted soil carbon estimation. That work has produced 12 peer-reviewed papers and a patented smartphone imaging technology.

I'm open to senior engineering and applied-ML roles.
